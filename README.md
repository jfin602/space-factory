# Space Factory

A 2D, top-down survival and automation game about one engineer keeping a spaceship alive through the factory built inside it.

You begin aboard a small stranded ship. Restoring power, producing supplies, and getting its engines running form the introduction. From there, you plan expeditions, explore space, land on planets and asteroids, confront hostile inhabitants, and establish surface factories that supply your ship on later visits.

The engineer has one life per run. Catastrophic ship damage or irreversible resource exhaustion and stranding can cause the engineer's death, permanently ending that run. Exact crash-survival rules remain an open design decision.

## Project status

## Core promises

1. Directly control a single engineer in a top-down 2D world.
2. Build the internal factory that supports your ship's operation and missions.
3. Learn by repairing the small stranded ship you start with.
4. Plan expeditions whose risks and supply demands matter.
5. Land, step outside, and connect surface factories to physical ship ports.
6. Revisit persistent outposts to collect resources and support further exploration.
7. Live with permanent consequences: the engineer can die and a run can be lost.

The current milestone is to turn this design into a small, testable playable sequence. See the roadmap for the proposed order; no development Phase is complete merely because its design is documented.
