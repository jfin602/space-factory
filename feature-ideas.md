## Maintenance

- Use a permanent `+` followed by a randomly assigned unused four-character ID from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`.
- Begin each entry with `### +ID — YYYY-MM-DD — Feature title`; preserve the original ID, date, and title.
- Record status, summary, intended behavior, value, relevant constraints, and open questions. Distinguish proposed implementation details from confirmed decisions.
- Recording an idea does not approve architecture, change the roadmap, or execute work.
- Move a completed idea to Shipped Ideas with a completion date, implementation summary, and linked evidence. Use an explicit deferred, rejected, or superseded disposition for other outcomes.
- Put defects in [known issues](known-issues.md), and do not duplicate the entire game roadmap in this log.
