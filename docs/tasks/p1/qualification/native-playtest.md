## Failure, repair, and limits

The first native fixture exposed a **FAIL** in label readability: with the P3 `(-60,-20)` offset, the upper hull crossed the title/mode; the longer development label also crossed the lower hull border. [Pre-repair capture](images/pre-repair-fixture-held.png) is retained. P4 changed only the fixture offset to `(-60,0)` and moved/shortened that label into upper-right space. The final check and native fixture were rerun and **PASS**. An auxiliary motion-capture wrapper later timed out while waiting for its child shell to exit after window closure; the window did close, and the separate final `p1-demo` launch, interaction route, and close exited 0. This helper timeout was not used as gameplay qualification evidence.

This qualification is for the native Linux development build and the selected ship/helm Gates. Windows/macOS play, exports, powered flight, factory supply, survival, saves, and the broader G0/G1 Gates are **NOT OBSERVED** here.
