## Maintenance

- Use a permanent, randomly assigned unused four-character ID from `ABCDEFGHJKLMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`.
- Begin each report with `### ID — YYYY-MM-DD — Issue title`; preserve the original ID, date, and title after resolution.
- Record status, summary, observed and expected behavior, source/platform context, impact, and available evidence. Label suspected causes as hypotheses.
- Put actual defects under Open Issues. Put new capabilities in [feature ideas](feature-ideas.md).
- Move resolved reports to Resolved Issues with a resolution date, fix summary, and linked verification. Keep original failures and any remaining evidence gaps.
- Deferred or superseded reports retain an explicit disposition. An empty log does not qualify the game as defect-free.
